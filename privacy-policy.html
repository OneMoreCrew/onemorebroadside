Privacy Policy

One More Broadside · One More Crew · Last updated: 22 September 2026

One More Broadside does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device.

Who we are

One More Broadside is made by One More Crew, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at hello@onemorecrew.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, we do not use analytics, we do not use advertising, and we do not track you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: banked gold, unlocked captains, cleared seas, secrets you have found, your settings and your lifetime statistics. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game removes it.

Internet access

The game is played offline. It makes no connection to our servers, because we do not have any. The only network requests it makes are to load its own music and sound files, and to reach Google Play when you make a purchase or restore one.

Purchases

If you buy the full version, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and tells the game whether the purchase was successful. We never see or receive your card details, your name or your address. Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because we collect no data at all, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide, change or delete. If you believe otherwise, please contact us and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that involve collecting data, such as crash reporting or online leaderboards, we will update this policy before those features are released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@onemorecrew.com.
