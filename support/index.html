The Full Voyage is locked after I bought it

On the title screen, open The Full Voyage and tap Restore Purchases. On iPhone and iPad you may be asked to sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Your progress is never deleted when the game locks or unlocks.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. If a refund goes through, the Full Voyage locks again; buying it again opens everything you had.

There is no sound on my iPhone

Like most iPhone games, One More Broadside follows the ring/silent switch. Turn silent mode off, turn the volume up, and check that Sound is On in the game's Settings.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. We never receive it. Our privacy policy says where it is kept on each device and how to remove it.
