Privacy Policy

One More Broadside · One More Crew · Last updated: 28 September 2026

One More Broadside does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game runs on your device, and nothing it saves is ever sent to us.

Who we are

One More Broadside is made by One More Crew, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. You can contact us at hello@onemorecrew.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, we do not use analytics, we do not use advertising, and we do not track you. The game has no accounts of its own, no sign-in and no online features.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: banked gold, unlocked captains, cleared seas, secrets you have found, your settings and your lifetime statistics. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Where it is kept, and how to remove it:

  • Windows, Linux and Steam: in your user profile's application data folder (on Windows, %APPDATA%\One More Broadside), in a saves folder. If the game ever hits an unexpected error it writes a short technical note to error.log in the same folder; it can include file paths on your computer (which may contain your user name), and it stays on your computer: it is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game does not delete this folder, so delete it yourself to remove your progress. If Steam Cloud is turned on, Steam keeps a copy of the saves folder in your Steam account.
  • Android and iOS: inside the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app removes it. If you have turned on your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud), your phone may include the game's data in that backup, as it does for other apps; we never receive it.
  • Web browser: in your browser's storage for the site the game is played on. Clearing that site's data removes it.

Internet access

The game is played offline. It makes no connection to our servers, because we do not have any. The only network requests it makes are to load its own music and sound files, and to reach the store you bought it from when you make or restore a purchase. If you play in a web browser, the website hosting the game receives the ordinary information any website receives when you visit it (such as your IP address); that site's own privacy policy covers it.

Purchases and platforms

We never see or receive your card details, your name or your address. Purchases are handled entirely by the store you buy from:

  • Google Play processes Android purchases and tells the game whether a purchase succeeded (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Apple's App Store does the same on iPhone and iPad (Apple Privacy Policy).
  • Steam handles purchases, achievements and Steam Cloud saves on PC (Valve Privacy Policy). The game tells the Steam client when you earn an achievement; it sends nothing else.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because we collect no data at all, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide, change or delete. If you believe otherwise, please contact us and we will look into it.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that involve collecting data, such as crash reporting or online leaderboards, we will update this policy before those features are released and change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@onemorecrew.com.
