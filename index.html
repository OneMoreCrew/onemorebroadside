How a voyage goes

You start alone in a small boat with one gun. Twenty minutes later you are either a warship the size of an island, or at the bottom of the sea.

1You steer

The guns aim and fire on their own. Your job is to sail through the gaps, grab what the wrecks leave behind, and keep the ship in one piece.

2Pick a card

Every level, three upgrades are dealt. Take a new weapon, make one stronger, or combine two into something far worse for whoever is chasing you.

3Meet what lives there

Every sea ends with a boss. The first is a squid the size of a harbour. The ones after that are worse.

Twenty captains

A pirate queen, a ghost whose victims join her crew, a professor with a lightning machine, a king who fires his own gold. Each one sails their own ships and brings one weapon nobody else has.

Seven seas

Tropical shallows, pack ice, a haunted swamp, a sea on fire, sunken marble ruins, a neon coast at sunset, and a sea of cloud that you cross on solar sails. Each has its own enemies, its own secrets and its own monster at the end.

Lose the ship, keep the gold

Sinking ends the voyage, not your progress. The gold you carried buys permanent upgrades and new captains, and every ship you have sailed goes on the shelf in a bottle, painted the way you like it.
