Description

One sentence

One More Broadside is a pirate survival roguelite where you only steer, and every gun on your ship fires by itself.

One paragraph

You start alone in a rowing boat with one gun. You steer; the guns aim and fire on their own. Sink ships, pick up what they drop, and every level choose one of three upgrade cards: a new weapon, a stronger one, or two combined into something much worse for the enemy. Your ship grows as you level, from a rowing boat to a man-of-war. Twenty minutes in, the sea's final boss arrives. Sink or win, the gold you carried buys permanent upgrades and new captains for the next voyage.

In numbers

  • 20 captains, each with their own ships and one weapon nobody else has
  • 7 seas, each with its own enemies, secrets and final boss
  • 40 weapons, 14 of which evolve into something bigger
  • 21 rival captains who come looking for you, and over 80 kinds of enemy
  • 22 songs on the ship's radio
  • No adverts, no timers and no currencies to buy, on any platform. On Android the first sea is free and one purchase unlocks the rest for good.

How it was made

One More Broadside was made by a solo developer with the help of AI tools, as its store pages disclose. The recorded songs and their singing voices were generated with the AI music service Mureka from our own prompts and lyric sheets. The 3D ships, sea creatures and props are built from code largely written by AI coding assistants under the developer's direction, and the sound effects are synthesised by code written the same way. The logo and key art were made with AI image generation. Everything was reviewed by the developer.
